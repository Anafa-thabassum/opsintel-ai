[0:00–0:20 | Open dashboard and a failed run]
When a data pipeline fails, engineers often have to piece together run status, row counts, and logs. OpsIntel brings those observations into one investigation and keeps the evidence attached to each finding.

[0:20–0:45 | Show the real Exasol query and result]
Exasol calculates completeness, rolling run history, and time-correlated downstream paths. The pipeline records here are synthetic demo telemetry. A downstream failure after an upstream one is a correlation to inspect, not proof of causation.

[0:45–1:10 | Show evidence references, row comparison, signals, and gaps]
For this run, these are the observed facts. OpsIntel reports the supported symptom and shows what evidence is missing. A candidate explanation is only a possibility; the system leaves the root cause unconfirmed.

[1:10–1:35 | Submit as investigator; show pending state]
An investigator can propose a specific cause with traceable supporting evidence. Submission creates a review request. The investigator cannot approve their own hypothesis.

[1:35–2:00 | Switch to a different authorized reviewer]
A separate authorized reviewer checks the source evidence, the class-specific criteria, and any alternatives or contradictions. Missing or conflicting support means more evidence is needed. Only a review that passes every required gate can create a confirmed memory.

[2:00–2:20 | Show read-only Exasol confirmation/audit rows; restart app]
Exasol stores the reviewed record and audit history. After restarting the application, the reviewer, decision, timestamp, and evidence references are still present.

[2:20–2:40 | Investigate a differently worded later incident]
This later incident uses different wording. OpsIntel retrieves a similar confirmed incident and shows the evidence on both sides. FastAPI computes the similarity score; it is a retrieval signal, not a probability. The new incident remains unconfirmed until it is reviewed on its own evidence.

[2:40–3:00 | Show evaluation limits]
Our current public issue comparison uses project-assigned labels, and the frozen reviewer set is small. We have no independent memory learning curve or measured engineer time savings. OpsIntel is designed to make the evidence and the human decision inspectable.
