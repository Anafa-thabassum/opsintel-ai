## Use restrictions and limitations

- None of these records has been added to the development or evaluation datasets.
- The Airflow #61130 PR is maintainer-approved cause evidence, but it is not a second independent taxonomy label.
- The Airflow #29049 cause statement is reporter-proposed and not independently verified here.
- The dbt issue's linked closure is not proof that the proposed OpsIntel category was independently labelled.
- Repository source-code licenses do not automatically establish a license for issue text. The source excerpt policy must be checked separately.
- No hidden 19-case holdout labels were read or changed. These records should not be used to claim independent evaluation without a separate label-adjudication process and an explicit frozen set.
